    Eggs → adult mosquitoes takes 7-14 days, then viral incubation and symptoms: rain 2-6 weeks back drives today's warning.

    Stress-test a ward: change recent weather and see how the 2-4 week forecast moves (runs in your browser, instant).

    Constrained resource allocation. Forecast cases → saline bags, diagnostic kits and beds per ward. When regional stock is short, it is shared by priority = risk tier × population density (weighted water-filling): the most urgent, densest wards reach 100% first and every ward still gets a share.

    Hospital surge & overflow routing. Wards whose bed demand exceeds local beds send patients to lower-burden wards in the same city only (least-risk, most-headroom first; receivers are filled to at most 90% so they keep a surge buffer). Anything that cannot be placed is a net city deficit.

    "What-if" vector-control simulator. Each crew (larvicide + fogging) removes part of a ward's breeding habitat (diminishing returns: 1 crew ≈ 29%, 4 crews ≈ 56% of the water-logging index). The changed features are run back through the same gradient-boosted models for +2, +3 and +4 weeks; the gap to the untouched forecast is the cases prevented.

    Multi-channel dispatch. Ward-specific notices built from fixed templates (no internet, no AI service): predicted cases, allocated vs required supplies, bed transfers, deficit alerts and assigned spraying crews.
