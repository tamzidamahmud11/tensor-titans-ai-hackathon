About Dengue Protirodh

A ward-level early-warning and community response ecosystem giving Bangladesh health teams and citizens a 2–4 week head start on dengue.

Why we built it

Dengue cases in Bangladesh consistently peak 2 to 4 weeks after heavy monsoon rains, yet municipal spraying and vector-control interventions traditionally deploy only after hospital wards overflow. Dengue Protirodh was created to bridge this critical operational gap: by forecasting risk for 60 wards across Bangladesh city corporations 2–4 weeks in advance, larvicide squads, drain clearing teams, and hospital saline logistics can be mobilized before the infection surge hits.

Core Capabilities & System Features

  • Multi-Week Lag Weather Intelligence: Analyzes rainfall lags of 0–7 weeks, rolling 2/4/8-week precipitation sums, diurnal temperature swings, and relative humidity against historical transmission curves.
  • Machine Learning Tree Ensembles: Evaluates gradient-boosted trees for expected counts alongside 10% and 90% quantile pinball loss models to deliver honest 80% prediction intervals.
  • Actionable Triage Protocol: Categorizes every ward into Low, Medium, High, or Critical risk tiers with clear field action recommendations and full CSV data export capabilities.
  • Interactive What-If Climate Simulation: Allows epidemiologists to test abnormal rainfall spikes (+150%) or heatwave shifts and see forecast adjustments instantaneously.
  • Community Action & Citizen Science: Connects early warnings with boots-on-the-ground volunteer cleanup drives, free fever screening clinics, educational webinars, and neighborhood discussion forums.

Our Engineering Philosophy

Honest, Leakage-Free Validation. Data is divided chronologically (Train 2016–2022, Validate 2023, Test 2024–2025). No random k-fold shuffling is used, preventing future information from artificially inflating validation scores.

Client-Side Edge Privacy. Prediction inference runs entirely on the user's hardware via lightweight JavaScript. No user data, custom uploaded CSVs, or session parameters are ever uploaded to remote servers.

Decision Support, Not Clinical Diagnosis. Dengue Protirodh is designed to guide vector control squads and municipal health planning. It is not an individual diagnostic tool. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

Built with

scikit-learn (offline gradient-boosted tree modeling), vanilla JavaScript (client-side inference engine), Plotly.js (interactive data visualizations), and pure CSS (supporting instant Dark/Light mode and English/Bengali localization). Full technical architecture is detailed in ARCHITECTURE.md.
