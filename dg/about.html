About Dengue Guard

A ward-level early-warning tool that gives health teams a few weeks' head start on dengue.

Why we built it

Dengue cases in Bangladesh rise weeks after the rain, yet response teams usually move only once hospital admissions climb. Dengue Guard was built to close that gap: it forecasts dengue risk for every ward 2–4 weeks ahead, so larvicide, drain clearing and hospital preparation can start while there is still time to make a difference.

What it does

  • Reads weather history (rainfall lags of 0–7 weeks, rolling sums, temperature and humidity) together with ward vulnerability and recent case trends.
  • Scores every ward with gradient-boosted tree models and shows an expected case count with an 80% prediction band.
  • Ranks wards into Low, Medium, High and Critical tiers, each with a recommended action, and lets you download the forecast as CSV.
  • Lets you test what-if weather scenarios and upload your own weekly data.

How we approach it

Honest validation. Data is split strictly by date: train 2016–2022, validate 2023, test 2024–2025. The test years are used once, for the final numbers shown in the dashboard.

Local and private. The model runs entirely in your browser. There are no API calls and no language model at prediction time, so your data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Decision support, not diagnosis. Forecasts help decide where to send teams first. They do not diagnose individuals. See the Terms.

Built with

scikit-learn (offline training), plain JavaScript for in-browser inference, and the bundled Plotly.js library for charts. Technical detail is in the architecture notes.
