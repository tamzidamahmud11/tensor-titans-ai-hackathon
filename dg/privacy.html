Privacy Policy

Short version: Dengue Guard runs in your browser and does not collect or send your data.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Summary

Dengue Guard is an offline, in-browser tool. We do not run a server that receives your data, we do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not set cookies.

2. Data you use in the dashboard

  • Built-in dataset. The bundled weekly ward dataset (2016–2025) is loaded from the files shipped with the app.
  • Your own CSV. If you upload a weekly CSV, it is read and processed by your browser only. It is not uploaded anywhere and is gone when you close or reload the page.
  • Downloads. The CSV forecast you download is created on your device.

3. What we do not do

  • No cookies, local storage or fingerprinting by the app.
  • No third-party analytics, ads or social-media pixels.
  • No API calls and no language model at prediction time.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we do not collect it.

4. Contact form and email

The contact form only opens your email app with a pre-filled message. If you send it, we receive the name, email address and message you chose to include, and we use them only to reply. Please do not include patient names or other personal health information.

5. Hosting and third parties

All code, data and charts (Plotly.js) are bundled with the app and load without external requests. If you host Dengue Guard on a web server, that server’s operator may keep ordinary access logs (such as IP address and time of visit), which are outside the app’s control.

6. Your data uploads and responsibility

Only upload data you are allowed to use. Weekly ward-level counts are expected; do not upload data that identifies individual patients.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: team@dengueguard.example or the Contact page.
