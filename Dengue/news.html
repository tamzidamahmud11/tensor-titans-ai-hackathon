    Dengue in the news

    Real reporting on Bangladesh's dengue seasons in 2025 and 2026, including the latest updates on vaccines, care and case counts (to 25 Nov 2026). Each card links to the original article. Share any story with the buttons below it.

    Cases
    The Daily Star · 25 Jul 2025 · 2025 season

    Dengue in 2025: July the deadliest month so far

    At least 28 people died in the first 25 days of July, taking the national toll to 70 with 18,789 cases. Doctors pointed to multi-organ failure, dengue shock syndrome and late hospital arrival, and urged care at the first warning signs.

    Hospitals
    Khaleej Times · 7 Sep 2026 · 2026 season

    Bangladesh dengue outbreak surges; 4 dead, 1,558 hospitalised in 24 hours

    The highest daily admissions of 2026 so far, bringing the year's total to 42,590 hospitalisations and 117 deaths. Experts feared September would be worse than August, with hospitals already stretched by a measles outbreak.

    Deaths
    Dawn / Reuters · 22 Sep 2025 · 2025 season

    Bangladesh sees worst single-day surge in dengue deaths

    Twelve deaths and 740 new admissions in one day pushed 2025 to at least 179 deaths and nearly 42,000 infections. Entomologists blamed a longer monsoon, urbanisation and poor waste management. 2023 remains the deadliest year, with 1,705 deaths.

    Admissions
    The Financial Express · 27 Sep 2025 · 2025 season

    Dengue hospitalisations top 45,000 in 2025

    With 45,206 admissions and 188 deaths so far, September was the worst month of the year: 13,730 admissions and 66 deaths by the 27th. Of 2,047 patients still in hospital, 747 were in Dhaka.

    Preparedness
    Scroll.in · 16 May 2026 · Analysis

    Bangladesh may be headed into another deadly dengue season – unprepared

    By Faisal Mahmud. Erratic heavy rain, uneven mosquito control and disrupted municipal services echo the conditions before 2023. Experts debate whether to plan for dengue vaccination, while others stress drainage and waste management.

    400 deathsin 2026
    News On AIR · 27 Oct 2026 · 2026 season

    11 dengue deaths in a single day; 400 fatalities this year

    The Directorate General of Health Services recorded 2,071 new infections, for a 2026 total of 128,237 cases. Eleven deaths in one day was the year's highest daily figure. Measles was also rising among children.

    Dhaka
    Prothom Alo · 20 Sep 2026 · Ward-level risk

    100 "hotspots" in North, 29 wards at high risk in South

    Dhaka North City Corporation mapped 100 dengue hotspots from the home addresses of patients admitted between 15 July and 31 August; Mirpur alone accounted for 607 of 1,678 patients. In the south, the corporation flagged 29 high-risk wards after finding Aedes larvae in 63 of its 75 wards. Experts warned that late September to early October could be the peak.

    Vaccines
    Reuters via TimesLive · 6 Oct 2026 · Vaccine progress

    Bangladesh considers dengue vaccine trials as outbreak worsens

    With all four dengue serotypes now circulating, the state health minister said Bangladesh is considering clinical trials of a dengue vaccine. The report noted over 90,350 hospitalisations and 281 deaths so far in 2026, and that any trial would need regulatory approval.

    Policy
    The Daily Star · 13 Oct 2026 · Policy update

    Govt to consult experts on dengue vaccine trials: State minister

    After a meeting with the Prime Minister, the government said it will consult experts on running local vaccine trials or reviewing existing research first. About 2,000 doctors received refresher training in dengue management, and the dengue death rate was said to be about 40 percent lower than three years ago.

    October
    Gulf Today · 1 Nov 2026 · 2026 season

    Dengue fever claims 418 lives in Bangladesh amid warnings of continued outbreak

    October was the deadliest month of 2026 with 173 deaths, ahead of September's 148. Experts warned the outbreak was unlikely to ease significantly before December.

    500+ deaths
    Xinhua · 16 Nov 2026 · 2026 season

    Bangladesh's dengue death toll tops 500

    DGHS data showed 164,107 cases and 502 deaths by 16 November. The report notes dengue is now spreading beyond its usual June to September season.

    Free tests
    The Financial Express · 18 Nov 2026 · Care & response

    Govt hospitals offering all dengue tests for free

    The state health minister told Parliament that all dengue tests are free at government hospitals, 2,000 doctors have had specialised dengue training, and community clinics are planned to become primary healthcare hubs.

    Qdenga
    Business Standard · 20 Jul 2026 · Vaccine progress

    Takeda gets DCGI nod for QDENGA, India's first dengue vaccine

    India's regulator approved Takeda's QDENGA for people aged 4 to 60, usable without prior dengue testing. A regional milestone as Bangladesh weighs its own vaccine options.

    526 deaths
    The Business Standard · 25 Nov 2026 · Latest count

    Dengue: Death toll at 526 as 2 more die

    In the 24 hours to 8am on 25 November, 1,152 patients were admitted and 4,861 remained in hospital. The 2026 total reached 177,137 cases, with 171,750 recoveries.
