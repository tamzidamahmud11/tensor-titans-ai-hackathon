⚠️ Critical Warning Signs

Seek emergency hospital care immediately if a patient shows:

  • Severe abdominal pain or tenderness
  • Persistent vomiting (>3 times in 24 hrs)
  • Bleeding from gums or nose
  • Lethargy, extreme restlessness, confusion
  • Sudden drop in body temperature accompanied by clammy skin
