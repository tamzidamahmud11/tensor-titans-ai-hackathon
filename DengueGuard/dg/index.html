From rainfall to ranked wards

Eggs to adult mosquitoes takes 7–14 days, then comes viral incubation. Rain several weeks back is a strong signal for today's cases, and the model is built around that delay.

1

Read the weather history

Rainfall lags of 0–7 weeks, rolling 2/4/8-week sums, plus temperature and humidity lags — using only data available before the warning date.

2

Score every ward

Gradient-boosted trees combine weather with recent case trends and ward vulnerability: density, water-logging and sanitation.

3

Rank and act

Wards are sorted into Low, Medium, High and Critical tiers with uncertainty bands, ready to download as CSV for field teams.

What each risk tier asks you to do

Every ward gets a tier and a recommended action in the dashboard.

Low

Routine surveillance. Keep weekly larvae checks and community awareness running.

Medium

Pre-position larvicide and clear drains in this ward; brief clinics on case definition.

High

Deploy larvicide and fogging teams within 7 days; reserve beds, saline and test kits.

Critical

Emergency response: door-to-door source reduction now, surge beds and saline, daily case reporting.

About Dengue Guard

A ward-level early-warning tool that gives health teams a few weeks' head start on dengue. It forecasts risk for 60 wards in 5 city corporations, runs privately in your browser and is built for decision support, not diagnosis.

Read more about us →

Questions

What does the forecast tell me?

The expected number of dengue cases per ward per week, 2–4 weeks ahead, with an 80% range and a risk tier. It guides where to send teams first. It does not diagnose individuals.

Which data does it use?

The hackathon dataset: 60 wards in 5 city corporations, 31,320 weekly records from 2016 to 2025, covering weather, ward vulnerability and reported cases. You can also upload your own weekly CSV.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Everything runs in your browser, with no server, no API call and no LLM at prediction time.

How accurate is it?

On the held-out 2024–2025 test years, R² is 0.73 at +3 weeks and 0.61 at +4 weeks, so longer warnings are less certain. The Model validation tab shows every metric.

Privacy Policy

Short version: Dengue Guard runs in your browser and does not collect or send your data.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

Dengue Guard is an offline, in-browser tool. We do not run a server that receives your data, we do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and the only cookie we set is a small first-party one that remembers your cookie choice.

Data you use in the dashboard

  • Built-in dataset. The bundled weekly ward dataset (2016–2025) is loaded from the files shipped with the app.
  • Your own CSV. If you upload a weekly CSV, it is read and processed by your browser only. It is not uploaded anywhere and is gone when you close or reload the page.
  • Downloads. The CSV forecast you download is created on your device.

What we do not do

  • No cookies or local storage other than the one that remembers your cookie choice, and no fingerprinting.
  • No third-party analytics, ads or social-media pixels. Share buttons are plain links, not embedded widgets.
  • No API calls and no language model at prediction time.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we do not collect it.

Cookies and your choices

When you first visit, a notice asks whether you allow optional cookies. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

  • dg_consent (strictly necessary, first-party, kept for 180 days): stores your cookie choice. A copy is kept in your browser's local storage as a fallback.
  • Preferences and analytics: Dengue Guard does not currently set any. If we add them, they will stay off unless you allow them.

Contact form and email

The contact form only opens your email app with a pre-filled message. If you send it, we receive the name, email address and message you chose to include, and we use them only to reply. Please do not include patient names or other personal health information.

Hosting and third parties

All code, data and charts (Plotly.js) are bundled with the app and load without external requests, with one exception: the News page shows photos loaded from the news publishers’ own servers, which can see your IP address when the images load. Share buttons on that page only open Facebook, X, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or your email app if you click them. If you host Dengue Guard on a web server, that server’s operator may keep ordinary access logs (such as IP address and time of visit), which are outside the app’s control.

Your data uploads and responsibility

Only upload data you are allowed to use. Weekly ward-level counts are expected; do not upload data that identifies individual patients.
