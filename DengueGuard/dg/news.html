Dengue in the news

Real reporting on Bangladesh's dengue seasons in 2025 and 2026. Each card links to the original article. Share any story with the buttons below it.

Dhaka
Prothom Alo · 20 Sep 2026 · Ward-level risk

100 "hotspots" in North, 29 wards at high risk in South

Dhaka North City Corporation mapped 100 dengue hotspots from the home addresses of patients admitted between 15 July and 31 August; Mirpur alone accounted for 607 of 1,678 patients. In the south, the corporation flagged 29 high-risk wards after finding Aedes larvae in 63 of its 75 wards. Experts warned that late September to early October could be the peak.

Why it matters here: this is exactly the ward-by-ward picture Dengue Guard forecasts.

Hospitals
Khaleej Times · 7 Sep 2026 · 2026 season

Bangladesh dengue outbreak surges; 4 dead, 1,558 hospitalised in 24 hours

The highest daily admissions of 2026 so far, bringing the year's total to 42,590 hospitalisations and 117 deaths. Experts feared September would be worse than August, with hospitals already stretched by a measles outbreak.

Deaths
Dawn / Reuters · 22 Sep 2025 · 2025 season

Bangladesh sees worst single-day surge in dengue deaths

Twelve deaths and 740 new admissions in one day pushed 2025 to at least 179 deaths and nearly 42,000 infections. Entomologists blamed a longer monsoon, urbanisation and poor waste management. 2023 remains the deadliest year, with 1,705 deaths.

Cases
The Daily Star · 25 Jul 2025 · 2025 season

Dengue in 2025: July the deadliest month so far

At least 28 people died in the first 25 days of July, taking the national toll to 70 with 18,789 cases. Doctors pointed to multi-organ failure, dengue shock syndrome and late hospital arrival, and urged care at the first warning signs.

Admissions
The Financial Express · 27 Sep 2025 · 2025 season

Dengue hospitalisations top 45,000 in 2025

With 45,206 admissions and 188 deaths so far, September was the worst month of the year: 13,730 admissions and 66 deaths by the 27th. Of 2,047 patients still in hospital, 747 were in Dhaka.

Preparedness
Scroll.in · 16 May 2026 · Analysis

Bangladesh may be headed into another deadly dengue season – unprepared

By Faisal Mahmud. Erratic heavy rain, uneven mosquito control and disrupted municipal services echo the conditions before 2023. Experts debate whether to plan for dengue vaccination, while others stress drainage and waste management.

400 deathsin 2026
News On AIR · 27 Oct 2026 · 2026 season

11 dengue deaths in a single day; 400 fatalities this year

The Directorate General of Health Services recorded 2,071 new infections, for a 2026 total of 128,237 cases. Eleven deaths in one day was the year's highest daily figure. Measles was also rising among children.
